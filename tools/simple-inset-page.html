Why enquiry
emails are not
reaching you

The form is not actually sending anything. When someone presses send, the website opens a half-written email inside their own email app and leaves it there for them to send.

Most people never finish it. Many have no email app set up at all. So the enquiry disappears, and you are never told. That is why your test never arrived.

What happens next

The site is currently on a free preview address, which can show pages but cannot send email on its own. Enquiry delivery is set up as part of moving the site to your own domain.

  1. 1
    Payment is cleared and the site moves to your own domain.
  2. 2
    We connect the enquiry form to a delivery service pointed at info@ntfint.com, or any inbox you prefer.
  3. 3
    We test it end to end and send you the test message so you can see it arrive.
  4. 4
    You check the inbox and the spam folder once, and mark that first message as “not spam” so the rest land normally.
  5. 5
    From then on every enquiry reaches you within seconds, with a copy kept online as a backup.
After that: every enquiry arrives in your inbox within seconds, and a copy is kept online in case an email ever goes missing. Keep WhatsApp on the site as well — most buyers in this trade reply there faster.

Adding a
new product

The website is not edited page by page. All the products live in one list, and the site rebuilds itself from that list — the collection tile, the product page, the search and the site map all appear on their own.

What we need from you

  1. 1
    One clear photo of the product, as big a file as you have.
  2. 2
    The name, and which of the five lines it belongs to.
  3. 3
    Sizes you make it in, and the minimum order quantity.
  4. 4
    Three or four facts a buyer asks about: fabric or weight, how it is built, how it can be branded.
  5. 5
    One or two lines describing it, in your own words. We will tidy them.

Send those over on WhatsApp and the product is live on the site the same day. A whole new collection works the same way — its name, a photo and its products.

Two things worth knowing

Nothing is ever lost

Every change is saved with a date, so anything can be put back exactly as it was.

Changes go live in a minute

Once a change is published, the site updates itself. Your visitors do nothing.
