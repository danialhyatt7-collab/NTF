The main menu

These six pages sit in the header on every screen. Everything else on the site is reached from one of them.

  • 01

    Home

    /index.html

    The front page. Shows who we are, the five product lines, the departments inside the factory and the nine-step process, then sends the visitor to a quote.

  • 02

    Collections

    /collections.html

    The catalogue. One tile per product line; clicking a tile opens that line's own page.

  • 03

    Process

    /process.html

    How an order is made, step by step, from your sketch to the packed carton.

  • 04

    About

    /about.html

    Who NTF INTERNATIONAL is, what the factory can do and the full manufacturing banner.

  • 05

    Contact

    /contact.html

    Phone, WhatsApp, email, address and working hours.

  • 06

    Request a Quote

    /quote.html

    The enquiry form: product line, sizes, quantity and how you want it branded.

Also on the site, but not in the menu: a Not Found page that catches a mistyped address and offers the catalogue, plus two files search engines read — an index of all pages and a crawler instruction file.

How a buyer gets to a quote

The whole site is built to carry someone from the front page to a filled-in enquiry in six moves.

  • 01

    Arrives

    A buyer lands on the home page, from search or a link you sent.

  • 02

    Browses

    They open Collections and pick the line they need — say MMA Wear.

  • 03

    Compares

    Inside the line they see its five products, each with fabric, sizes and build detail.

  • 04

    Reads the detail

    A product page gives the full specification and the minimum order.

  • 05

    Checks you out

    Process and About answer the question every buyer asks: can this factory actually make it.

  • 06

    Asks for a price

    Request a Quote collects the line, sizes, quantity and branding, and emails it to you.

Every product page and every collection page carries a “request a quote” button, so a buyer never has to go looking for it.
