How we work

Brief to bulk

No portals, no onboarding calls. A short, honest sequence that most clients complete in four to six weeks from first message to delivered cartons.

01

Brief & costing

Send artwork, a tech pack or a reference product. Within one working day you get per-unit pricing at two or three quantity breaks, fabric options and a lead time.

02

Pattern & mock-up

We build the pattern and send a digital mock-up with your artwork placed, so placement and proportion are agreed before anything is cut.

03

Physical sample

A pre-production sample ships in 7 to 10 working days with tracking. Wear it, wash it, pull on it. Tell us what to change.

04

Revision & sign-off

Fit or spec changes are applied and re-sampled where needed. Bulk starts only once you approve in writing.

05

Production & QC

3 to 4 weeks for most bulk runs. Every piece is measured and inspected, and we share line photos mid-run.

06

Packing & freight

Poly-bagged, tagged and boxed your way, then shipped door-to-door by air or sea — our forwarder or yours.

Good to know

The details that
save you weeks

Sampling charges

Samples are charged at cost plus courier and credited against your first bulk invoice.

Payment terms

Typically 50% to start production and 50% against shipping documents. Bank transfer or Wise.

Repeat orders

Patterns and artwork stay on file, so a reorder skips straight to production with no sampling delay.

Small changes mid-run

Colour or label changes are usually fine before cutting starts. After that they cost time, so we flag it honestly rather than quietly missing your date.

Ready when you are

Let’s build your line

Send us your artwork, tech pack or even a rough sketch. You get costing and a sampling plan back within one working day.
