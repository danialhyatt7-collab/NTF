MMA Wear

MMA kit lives or dies on stretch recovery and seam strength. We build fight wear on four-way stretch knits and sublimated polyester, bond the panels where stitching would chafe, and flatlock everything that touches skin. Full-bleed art, team names and sponsor blocks are printed into the fabric rather than onto it, so nothing cracks or peels.
